Sample sequence

A 10-minute morning flow

Child's Pose

Kneel and fold forward, arms extended. Settle here for eight slow breaths before moving.

Cat–Cow

On hands and knees, move between arching and rounding the spine with each breath, ten rounds.

Downward Dog

Press hips up and back. Pedal the feet to warm up the calves and hamstrings.

Low Lunge, both sides

Step one foot forward, sink the back hip down. Hold five breaths, then switch sides.

Seated Forward Fold

Sit with legs extended, fold from the hips, not the back. Close the flow here for five breaths.
