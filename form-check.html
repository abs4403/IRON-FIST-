— Tracked angle
    How this works: a pose-detection model (MoveNet, via TensorFlow.js) finds your joints from the camera feed and measures the angles between them — for example your hip–knee–ankle angle on a squat. It compares those angles against simple form thresholds for the exercise you picked and tells you what to fix. It's a training aid, not a coach or a medical device — trust your own judgment and stop if something hurts.

    Privacy: the video never leaves your device. There's no server here — everything runs locally in your browser tab, and closing the tab clears it.

    Requirements: a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox) with camera access, and the page served over HTTPS — which GitHub Pages does automatically — or opened on localhost. It will not work from a bare double-clicked file.
